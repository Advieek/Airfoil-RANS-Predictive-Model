AirfRANS — NeurIPS 2022 Benchmark

A neural surrogate for RANS airfoil flow fields

Give it an airfoil shape, a Reynolds number, and an angle of attack. It predicts the full steady-state RANS flow field around the airfoil — velocity, pressure, turbulent viscosity — in milliseconds instead of the minutes-to-hours an OpenFOAM solve takes, then integrates the surface fields into lift and drag coefficients.

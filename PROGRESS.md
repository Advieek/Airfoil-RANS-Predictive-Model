- **BUG FOUND AND FIXED during Gate 6**: initial run on NACA 2412 @ Re 4e6/AoA 5° gave nonsensical Cl=-0.055, Cd=-0.063 (wrong sign/magnitude) — the predicted surface pressure was physically inverted (high pressure on the *upper* surface, suction on the *lower* surface, backwards from a lifting airfoil). Root cause: I generated *outward*-pointing surface normals, but verified against real training data (`data/cache/scarce_train.pt`, topmost point of a real sim) that AirfRANS's `normals` feature is **inward**-pointing (into the solid) — e.g. the topmost point (clearly upper surface) has normal ≈(0,-1), pointing down into the body. Since normals are one of the 7 model *input* features, feeding outward normals put every surface query out-of-distribution and corrupted predictions near the airfoil. Fixed `surface_normals()` to point inward to match, and correspondingly fixed: (a) the near-surface dense-sampling offset direction (must step outward = `-normal`), (b) the wall-shear finite-difference offset direction in predict.py (same fix), (c) the pressure-force sign in `integrate_forces` (force = `-p*n_outward` = `+p*n_inward` once normals are inward). After the fix, pressure field is physically correct: strong suction peak on the upper surface near the leading edge, positive/stagnation-like pressure on the lower surface near the leading edge.
- After fix: NACA 2412 @ Re 4e6/AoA 5°: **Cl = 0.618** (within the expected plausible 0.5–1.0 range). **Cd = -0.0205** — right order of magnitude (~0.02) but wrong sign. Root cause is *not* a new integration bug (isolated pressure-only vs viscous-only contributions: viscous Fv≈0.23N is negligible next to pressure Fp≈169N, so the sign error is entirely in the pressure-drag integral) — it's the same per-point-MLP drag weakness already established quantitatively in Gate 5 (Cd Spearman -0.19 on the real test set, matching the published paper's -0.139 for the same model class). A context-free per-point MLP cannot reliably resolve the fine pressure asymmetries that determine the (much smaller, harder-to-resolve) drag signal, even though it nails the coarser lift signal. Documenting honestly rather than chasing further: Cd sign should improve with the GraphSAGE upgrade (Step 9).
